A galaxy morphology classifier paired with a RAG explanation layer. Upload an image of a galaxy — a fine-tuned ResNet-18 predicts its morphological class with Top-3 confidence, then a retrieval pipeline pulls relevant astronomical context and a Groq-hosted Llama 3.3 70B writes a plain-language explanation of what you're looking at.

Pipeline
  • ResNet-18 fine-tuned on Galaxy10 DECals
  • FAISS semantic search over astronomy knowledge
  • Groq Llama 3.3 70B generates the explanation
  • Deployed as a public Streamlit app

A retrieval-augmented Q&A system over SEC 10-K filings. Documents are chunked, embedded with HuggingFace sentence-transformers, and indexed in FAISS — questions get answered by a locally-hosted Mistral model grounded in the retrieved filing text, served over a REST API.

Pipeline
  • PDF loading & chunking of 10-K filings
  • HuggingFace embeddings → FAISS index
  • Mistral (via Ollama) generates grounded answers
  • Custom eval: retrieval relevancy + answer faithfulness
