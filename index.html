PRJ_04 / 2026 / MOTORSPORT AI + SIMULATION

Undercut

Live & deployed
15K+ Real race laps modeled
10K Monte Carlo simulations
0.35s Tire deg. MAE
Live Demo → View Code

A Monte Carlo pit-strategy simulator that quantifies whether a given pit call was optimal — in expected seconds and positions gained or lost. Tire degradation curves are fitted per circuit and compound using fixed-effects regression on real FastF1 lap data, correcting for fuel burnoff and driver skill. Safety car probability is modeled at decile resolution. Thousands of race simulations run per candidate pit lap to surface the optimal strategy under uncertainty.

Pipeline
  • FastF1 API → 15,000+ real race laps across 4 circuits
  • Fixed-effects regression for tire degradation modeling
  • Circuit-specific safety car hazard model at decile resolution
  • Monte Carlo simulator across candidate pit strategies
  • Deployed as interactive Streamlit app
